### Reference usage flow

1. Start with clean, dry skin and remove makeup and residue.
2. Long-press the power button, select one of seven modes with the mode control, and choose one of five intensity levels.
3. Secure the adjustable head strap and use the device for its 10-minute automatic session.
4. Apply a serum or moisturizer after the session.

The ProTouch FAQ says multiple modes may be used in separate sessions with a short break between treatments. It advises sensitive-skin users to begin at a lower intensity and increase according to comfort.
