Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered.

How long should a session last?

A session can last between 10 and 30 minutes.

How often can the mask be used?

Follow the session frequency in your included instruction booklet. Contact SKINTRONICS if you need help with the instructions.

Can infrared be used with the other lights?

Yes. Infrared can be turned on with each light mode.

Yes. A full charge takes around two hours and provides around ten hours of battery use.
